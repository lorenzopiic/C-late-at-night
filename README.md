# Concept
This is a study repository that collects all the programs that address the fundamental areas I am gradually studying as I delve deeper into computer science. 
## Why C
I continue to program in C because I prefer to focus on understanding concepts without having to switch between languages and address the syntactic and paradigmatic differences that each would introduce.
In this sense, I feel it's a good idea to focus on the content for a long time and less on the container in this case, but there will certainly come a time when I will explore many other languages.
## Always learning
I believe that the learning path is continuous, but not endowed with a continuity that is comparable to a continuous flow, perfect in its flow and always, in some way "complete".
I will take the liberty of publishing unfinished, poorly functioning, poorly structured, or unstructured content. This is because I believe that in this area retracing one's steps, reviewing one's ideas and reorganizing them, questioning them without manias for perfectionism reflects not a flaw, but a great quality.
I'm aware that the overall look may seem messy or nonsensical, but in the detail of each individual implementation I will try to express a love of learning, in the freest sense of the word
